## What changed and why

The founder's existing work sample — *The Rise of Mobile Money in Nigeria* (14pp, July 2026) —
is a competent, properly-sourced market research report with a methodology section, a limitations
section, traceable institutional sources, stakeholder-segmented recommendations, and at least two
genuine analytical insights (the value-vs-volume divergence; the penetration-vs-rank reframe).

**Consequence:** the credential gap is substantially neutralised. The original plan led with
document localization because it has no credential gate. That reasoning assumed no portfolio.
The portfolio exists, and it is a *research report* — so the entry offer must also be research.
A prospect must never see a mismatch between the sample and the thing being sold.

**Localization does not disappear.** It becomes (a) the marketplace revenue line, where buyers
are already searching for EN⇄FR work, and (b) a premium *upgrade* on every research deliverable.

## Ideal Customer Profile

**Primary ICP — "the operator with a West Africa decision to make"**

| Attribute | Definition |
|---|---|
| Who | Expansion lead, country manager, strategy lead, or founder at a company entering or operating in West Africa; principal at a 2–20 person Africa-focused consultancy; programme manager at an NGO working in WAEMU countries |
| Company size | 5–200 people. Big enough to have a budget, small enough that one person decides. |
| Trigger event | Considering a market entry · writing a board or investor memo · bidding on donor work · building a partner or competitor map · needs a French-language deliverable |
| Budget authority | Can approve $200–$2,000 without a procurement process |
| Where they are | LinkedIn (searchable by title and company), marketplaces, industry newsletters |
| Disqualify | Pre-funding founders · students · anyone who needs it "by tomorrow" · anyone who opens with a request to lower the price |

**Secondary ICP — "the consultancy that needs bilingual desk capacity"** — the retainer buyer.
Same people, different purchase: continuous, white-labelled research support.

## The pain, in the buyer's words

- "We need to decide whether to enter Côte d'Ivoire and everything credible is in French."
- "Our investor memo is due Friday and nobody here can write the market section."
- "The consultancy quoted us €15,000. We need something for two thousand."
- "We won the bid but the deliverable has to be submitted in French."
- "I spent nine hours on a partner map that a researcher should have done."

## Desired outcome

**A decision the buyer can defend to their board, in the language they need it in, at a price
that does not require approval.** They are not buying pages. They are buying the ability to say
"here's why we're doing this, and here's the source" without spending their own week on it.

## The offer ladder

### Tier 1 — Market Snapshot `$150–$250` · 5 business days `ENTRY / VALIDATION`

- 5–8 pages answering **one specific question** ("Who are the licensed payment providers in Benin
  and what does each need to enter?")
- Key figures with named institutional sources · one comparison chart · limitations stated
- Delivered as a formatted PDF + editable doc
- **Risk reversal: full refund if it isn't useful.** At this price the downside is bounded and it
  removes the "who are you" objection entirely. **Only offered on this tier.**
- **This tier exists to break the seal, not to make money.** One sale hits the month-1 $100 target.

### Tier 2 — Market Brief `$600–$900` · 10 business days `CORE PRODUCT`

- 15–25 pages, the structure of the existing mobile money sample: market size and data, key
  players and structure, barriers, international comparison, stakeholder recommendations, outlook,
  limitations, sourced appendix tables
- One revision round · 30-minute walkthrough call
- **Payment: 50% deposit, 50% on delivery.** No refund tier — the deposit is the qualification.

### Tier 3 — Bilingual Market Brief `$900–$1,400` · 12 business days `THE MOAT`

- Everything in Tier 2, delivered in **both English and French**, each version written natively —
  not translated
- **This is the only tier no competitor can copy**, and the only one that uses the founder's
  actual unfair advantage. Price it as the default recommendation, not the upsell.

### Tier 4 — Research Retainer `$500–$1,500/month` · rolling `RECURRING REVENUE`

- An agreed monthly volume — e.g. 2 snapshots, or 1 brief, or a fixed number of research hours
- White-labelled to the client's template where they want it
- Monthly invoice, 3-month minimum, 30 days notice
- **This is the line that reaches $3,000/month.** Three retainers, not thirty projects.

### Standalone: EN⇄FR Document Localization `$150–$600/document`

Investor decks, reports, grant applications, financial documents. Sold on marketplaces where
buyers are already searching, and offered to every research client who has documents to move
between languages. Fast, high-margin, requires no credential.

| Upsell | Price | When to offer |
|---|---|---|

## Subscription opportunity — and its blocker

The quarterly refresh is the first genuine recurring product, and it works on **invoice**, which
matters because Stripe is unavailable in Nigeria. A self-serve card subscription (the eventual
WAEMU regulatory tracker) stays blocked until payment rails are solved — see D-005 and E0.

## Onboarding — 6 steps, no software required

1. **Intake form** (free form tool): what decision are you making · which market · deadline · what
   you already know · what would make this useful
2. **Scoping message or 15-min call**: confirm the question is answerable with secondary data.
   If it needs fieldwork, say so and decline. **Declining out-of-scope work is a selling behaviour.**
3. **One-page proposal**: the question, the deliverable, the date, the price. No boilerplate.
4. **Deposit invoice** (50%, or 100% on Tier 1). Work starts when money moves. No exceptions.
5. **Mid-point check-in** at day 3 or 5: "here's the shape of what I'm finding" — catches
   misalignment before it becomes a rewrite, and it is the single strongest retention behaviour.
6. **Delivery**: PDF + editable doc + walkthrough + one revision round. Then ask for the
   testimonial *the same day*, while the relief is fresh.

## Delivery workflow — Tier 2 in 10 days (~14 working hours)

| Day | Step | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope lock: define the question precisely, list the sources that could answer it | 1.5 |
| 2–4 | Source collection: institutional data (central banks, statistics offices, IMF/FRED, GSMA, regulators), French-language primary sources | 4 |
| 5 | Structure and data tables; identify the reframe — *what does this data say that the buyer does not expect?* | 2 |
| 5 | **Mid-point check-in with client** | 0.5 |
| 6–8 | Write: exec summary last, limitations honestly, recommendations segmented by stakeholder | 4 |
| 9 | Fact-check every headline number against primary source. Format. | 1.5 |
| 10 | Deliver, walk through, request testimonial | 0.5 |

**Non-negotiable quality rules** (these are what make the work resellable):
every headline figure carries a named source · limitations are stated, never hidden · projections
are labelled as scenarios, not forecasts · at least one insight that reframes the buyer's
assumption. The existing sample already does all four — that is the standard to hold.

## Economic model

**Planning assumptions — replace with measured data after 100 outreach messages.**

```
100 outreach/week  →  30 accepted  →  6 replies  →  4 conversations
                   →  1.5 qualified  →  0.8 calls  →  ~0.2 customers/week
```

≈ **1 customer/month from cold LinkedIn alone.** Marketplaces run in parallel precisely because
they collapse the top of this funnel — those buyers are already searching.

### Customers needed per revenue level

| Monthly target | Snapshots ($200) | Briefs ($750) | Bilingual ($1,100) | Retainers ($900) | Realistic mix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $100 | **1** | — | — | — | **1 snapshot — the month-1 goal** |
| $500 | 3 | 1 | — | — | 1 brief, or 2 snapshots + 1 localization job |
| $1,000 | 5 | 1–2 | 1 | 1 | 1 bilingual brief |
| $3,000 | 15 ⚠️ | 4 | 3 | **3** | 2 retainers + 1 bilingual brief |
| $5,000 | ❌ | 7 ⚠️ | 5 | 5 | 3 retainers + 2 bilingual briefs |
| $10,000 | ❌ | ❌ | 9 ❌ | 11 ❌ | **Requires subcontracted delivery or 2× prices** |

⚠️ at or beyond solo capacity once selling time is counted · ❌ not achievable solo

**The structural conclusion:** the path to $3,000/month runs through **retainers and the bilingual
tier**, not through selling more cheap reports. Volume is a trap; every additional project costs
selling time as well as delivery time.

## Immediate asset gap — highest-leverage action available

The existing sample proves West African research capability in English. It does **not** prove the
bilingual claim, which is the entire moat.

**Produce a French version of the mobile money report.** The research is already done, so the
marginal cost is a few hours, and it converts one asset into a matched EN/FR pair that proves the
one thing no competitor can claim. Nothing else available for $0 comes close to this in value.

Fix at the same time: remove the "Economic Research Analyst" byline (D-003), remove "Final Version
(Verified)" from the cover, and re-verify every headline figure against its primary source.

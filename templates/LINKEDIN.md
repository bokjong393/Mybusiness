# LinkedIn Profile Copy

The profile is not a résumé — it is the landing page for every connection request you send. Someone accepts, glances at the profile, and decides in about eight seconds whether the free sample you're about to send is worth reading.

**Rule: describe the service, never the background.** Nothing here is untrue — you simply lead with what you do rather than what you are.

---

## Headline

> Buying-signal research for B2B outbound agencies — 50 verified prospects per ICP, every row dated and sourced. Free 3-row sample, no pitch.

Under the 220-character limit. It states the buyer, the deliverable, the differentiator, and the offer in one line.

## Profile settings

| Item | Do this | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Photo** | A real, clear headshot. Plain background, face filling most of the frame. | The single largest trust lever you have, and it's free. A profile with no photo reads as a bot. |
| **"Open to work" banner** | **Off.** | It signals job-seeking. You are a vendor, not a candidate — the two read completely differently to an agency owner. |
| **Location** | Leave it accurate. | Don't hide it, don't lead with it. Evasion confirms the fear; a normal, complete profile does not invite the question. |
| **Banner image** | Plain colour or a simple line of text: *"Verified buying signals for B2B agencies."* | Free reinforcement. Anything cluttered looks worse than nothing. |
| **Featured section** | Add the redacted sample file the day you have one. | This becomes the highest-converting asset on the profile. |
| **Experience** | One entry: your own service, started this month. Describe the deliverable. | Honest, and it stops the profile looking empty. Do not invent history. |

## What not to do

- **Don't claim clients you don't have.** One agency owner asking "who have you worked with?" and catching a bluff ends the relationship and the referral chain behind it.
- **Don't list "Economics student" in the headline.** True, and irrelevant to the buyer. It belongs in Education, where it's fine.
- **Don't optimize this twice.** Set it once, then go and contact people. Re-editing the profile is the most comfortable form of procrastination available to you.

# Mybusiness — AI Digital Product Research

Working repo for Peace Sossa. Research and launch plans for a profitable AI digital
product business, built around constraints that are actually mine: student time,
low capital, Nigeria + Benin, English and French.

## The one-line thesis

The profitable gap is not "an AI product." It is **AI knowledge delivered in French to
West African business owners** — a market of 250M+ people that English-language
platforms have not reached, where I have a language and culture advantage that is
genuinely hard to copy.

Everything else in here either funds that or supports it.

## The plan in three moves

| Move | What | Purpose | Timeline |
|------|------|---------|----------|
| Cash engine | Bilingual WhatsApp AI setup for small businesses | Money now, proof, real customer language | Weeks 1–6 |
| Asset engine | `L'IA pour ton business` — French AI course + toolkit | Scales without my hours | Weeks 4–14 |
| High-value service | French donor-report writing for NGO consultants | Best rate per hour, uses my degree | Test from week 8 |
| Leverage | Localize proven Nigerian courses into French on rev-share | Sell products I did not have to build | Opportunistic |

Note on the third row: this is deliberately a **service**, not a software product.
Francophone M&E software already exists and is regionally entrenched — see
[`docs/competitive-reality.md`](docs/competitive-reality.md). I am not building a platform.

## The number that sets expectations

Selar paid **₦18 billion to 400,000 creators** last year. That averages **~₦45,000 per
creator per year** — about ₦3,750 a month. Most creators earn close to nothing; a small
top slice earns nearly all of it.

The lesson is not "digital products don't work." It is that the product was never the
hard part. **Distribution is the hard part**, and that is what these plans optimise for.

## Two rules taken from outside advice

1. **Sell the outcome, not the AI.** Three levels of product: AI-*generated* files (PDFs,
   prompt packs — copyable, no moat), AI-*powered* systems (template + workflow + AI), and
   AI *agents* that complete a job end to end. Never build at level one.
2. **Deliver manually to 5–10 paying customers before building anything.** Whatever I end
   up repeating identically every time *is* the product. If nothing repeats, I still have
   a business, and I have lost no build time finding out.

## The question that replaces "what can AI make?"

> What annoying, expensive task are people already paying humans to do?

And its companion, learned the hard way in
[`docs/competitive-reality.md`](docs/competitive-reality.md): "does this already exist?"
is almost always yes, and is the wrong question. **Uniqueness is not the asset. Access is.**

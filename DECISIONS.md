# DECISIONS.md — Strategic Decision Log

Format: decision · date · status · rationale · what would reverse it.

---

## D-001 · LinkedIn is an outbound channel, not a publishing channel `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** 55 followers, 150 impressions/week, 28 profile views in 90 days. Content-to-inbound
requires thousands of engaged followers and 6–12 months. Direct search-and-message requires zero
followers and works this week.
**Reverses if:** followers pass ~1,500 with meaningful engagement and inbound DMs appear organically.

## D-002 · Positioning is the anglophone/francophone border, not "economic research" `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** the certifications (Excel, Power BI, Tableau, MySQL, CFA Investment Foundations) are
commodity — hundreds of thousands hold them. Native EN/FR bilingualism combined with Nigeria+Benin
context is not replicable by a competitor and not learnable quickly.
**Reverses if:** outreach shows buyers value the analytics skills more than the language/region edge.

## D-003 · Do not lead with the "Economic Research Analyst" title `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** the founder is a second-year BSc student. Any buyer who checks finds a gap between
title and record, and the sale dies on trust rather than on capability. Work samples carry the
credibility instead.
**Reverses if:** never — sell the specific work, not the implied seniority. Revisit on graduation.

## D-004 · Services before software; manual delivery before automation `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** <$100 budget, no validated demand, no commercial track record. Software built now
would be built on guesses. Google Docs delivered on deadline is the entire product for 60 days.
**Reverses if:** the same research is being sold repeatedly to different buyers — then productize.

## D-005 · Payment rails are Blocker #1, ahead of all selling `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** Stripe does not onboard Nigeria-registered businesses, and the founder confirms no
rail has ever actually paid out to them. A closed deal that cannot be collected burns a warm buyer
and the reputation with them. Verified by receiving real money, not by reading a website.
**Reverses if:** never. This is a precondition.

## D-006 · Marketplaces run in parallel with LinkedIn for the first 30 days `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** founder has never been paid by a client and has one work sample. Marketplaces supply
buyers who are already searching, handle payment collection, and produce a public review — solving
discovery, rails, and proof simultaneously. LinkedIn remains the long-term brand channel.
**Reverses if:** marketplace economics prove unworkable (fees, competition, payout blocked), or once
LinkedIn outbound sustains its own pipeline.

## D-007 · Do not build a business selling to students or peers `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** the founder's natural network is students and early-career peers — real pain, no
budget. This is the most common failure mode for a founder monetizing their own demographic.
**Reverses if:** an institution (university, donor) will pay *on behalf of* students. Then the
customer is the institution, not the student.

## D-008 · The ladder: Localization → Briefs → Retainers → Data product `2026-08-29` · **PROPOSED**

**Rationale:** highest-scoring executable models (7.55 / 7.30 / 7.15) sold to overlapping buyers, so
distribution effort compounds. Each rung funds the next. Recurring revenue arrives via retainers,
not via a subscription product that current payment rails cannot support.
**Status:** awaiting founder confirmation before Phase 5 offer design.
**Reverses if:** E2/E3 fail — reply rates below threshold or no named projects in 10 conversations.

## D-009 · $3,000/month is a month 6–9 target, not a 30-day target `2026-08-29` · **DECIDED**

**Rationale:** capacity math — ~96 billable hours/month at steady state means $3,000/month requires
~$31/hour effective, which is a pricing and retention outcome, not a volume one. Reaching it needs
3 retainers or equivalent, and retainers require delivered projects first.
**Reverses if:** an unusually large first client compresses the timeline. Do not plan for it.

# DECISIONS.md — Strategic Decision Log

Each entry records what was decided, what it was decided *against*, and what evidence would reverse it. **A decision without a reversal condition is a belief, not a decision.**

---

## D1 — Sell a service, not software *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** Start as a manual research service. Write no code until the Phase 11 gates are met.
**Alternatives rejected:** micro-SaaS for signal monitoring (O17); a paid niche database (O14).
**Reasoning:** with a 60-day revenue deadline and under $100 of capital, building before selling is the highest-probability failure mode available. Software is the destination, not the entry.
**Reverses if:** 3+ retained clients, $2,000+ MRR, and a repeated manual step that automation would save 10+ hrs/month on.

## D2 — Sell to agencies, not end clients *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** Target 2–15 person B2B lead-gen agencies.
**Alternatives rejected:** SaaS companies directly; nonprofits (O8); recruiters (O3, held as Plan C).
**Reasoning:** one decision-maker, no procurement, publicly listed and reachable, already spending on the problem, resells our work so location is invisible, and structurally cannot object to cold outreach. This combination neutralizes almost every disadvantage of a cold-start founder.
**Reverses if:** under 3 serious leads from 200 contacts. Then switch buyer (recruiters first), not skill.

## D3 — Sell verified signals, not lists *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** Every row carries a signal, a date, and a source URL. Never sell an unqualified list.
**Alternatives rejected:** volume list-building at commodity prices.
**Reasoning:** research shows list-building is commoditized by AI, while qualification and validation still command human rates. The citation *is* the product — it is what a nervous first-time buyer can verify in ten seconds.
**Reverses if:** clients demonstrably don't value citations and buy on volume alone. (Would be a serious blow to the thesis, not a small pivot.)

## D4 — Proof-first outreach as the core mechanic *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** Deliver 3 free researched rows inside the first cold message, before any ask.
**Alternatives rejected:** conventional cold pitching; building an audience first; marketplace-only.
**Reasoning:** simultaneously solves the credential gap (student), the trust gap (unknown vendor), and the location penalty. Finished work cannot be argued with. Made economical by building a reusable pool once and distributing from it.
**Reverses if:** reply rate stays under 3% across 200 contacts on two different message versions.

## D5 — Price the entry offer at $147 *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** $147 pilot, $450–650/month retainer.
**Alternatives rejected:** $47 (signals low quality, attracts bad clients); $500+ entry (triggers a meeting and a decision process).
**Reasoning:** below the threshold where an agency founder needs anyone else's approval. Against a market where alternatives cost $2,500+/month, it is an obvious yes for them and excellent revenue for us.
**Reverses if:** close rate holds at $197+ (raise), or serious leads consistently stall on price (rethink).

## D6 — Payment rail before outreach *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** Payoneer or Grey live before the first message goes out. The domiciliary account is a settlement destination only.
**Reasoning:** "wire money to my Nigerian bank account" converts a closed sale into a security conversation. The rail must exist before the question is asked, not after.
**Reverses if:** never. This is a hygiene decision.

## D7 — Weight speed over defensibility in scoring *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** The scoring model weights speed-to-revenue (12) and acquisition ease (12) far above defensibility (3).
**Reasoning:** with a 60-day deadline, an undefended business earning $800/month beats a defensible one earning $0. **This bias is stated openly so it can be challenged.**
**Reverses if:** the goal changes to $10k/month over 24 months. Then O2 (tender monitoring) wins outright and this whole document should be re-derived.

## D8 — Avoid academic-support work entirely *(2026-08-29)*

**Decision:** Do not pursue O24, despite genuine demand and good skill fit.
**Reasoning:** most real demand in that category is for work that constitutes academic misconduct, which carries direct risk to the founder's own standing as an enrolled student. Not worth it at any price.
**Reverses if:** never.

# FAILURES.md — What Didn't Work

The most valuable file here once it fills up. A failure recorded is an experiment that keeps paying; a failure forgotten gets repeated at full price.

**Rules:**
1. Record failures the day they happen, while the details are still uncomfortable and accurate.
2. Record the *number*, not the feeling. "Low reply rate" is useless; "4 replies from 185 contacts" is data.
3. Separate **the idea failed** from **the execution failed**. Most early failures are execution, and killing a good idea over bad execution is the expensive mistake.
4. Record near-misses too — a deal that almost closed teaches more than one that never started.

## Pre-mortem — failures predicted before they happen

Written on 2026-08-29, before launch, so that they can be recognized fast rather than rationalized slowly.

| # | Predicted failure | Early warning sign | Planned response |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Outreach never reaches volume; time goes into "preparing" | Day 10 arrives with under 60 contacts sent | Cut all setup work. Contacts are the only permitted activity. |
| 2 | Domain burns from sending too fast | Bounce rate above 3%, replies collapse | Stop email. Move to LinkedIn. Re-warm on a fresh domain. |
| 3 | Free samples get taken, nobody buys | 5+ people take rows, 0 quotes accepted | Cap free rows at 3. Quote by the second exchange, not the fourth. |
| 4 | Delivery takes triple the estimate, margin collapses | First pack exceeds 10 hours | Fix the process before selling another. Do not sell more of a broken delivery. |
| 5 | "We already use Clay" ends conversations | 3+ prospects say it unprompted | Reframe from capability to verification and hours saved. If it persists, the wedge is wrong — escalate to a real repositioning. |
| 6 | Exams destroy the schedule mid-experiment | A week passes under 10 hours | Plan around the academic calendar. Never sell capacity an exam week would break. |
| 7 | First client churns at month 2 | No reply to the month-2 check-in | Diagnose honestly: was it quality, price, or their own cash flow? Only one of those is ours to fix. |

**Reviewed monthly.** Predictions that come true get moved above with real numbers attached.

14 days, no argument

If a paid licence has not earned its price back on your next client, email hello@example.com within 14 days of buying it and we refund you in full. You do not need to justify it. You will not be asked to jump through hoops or sit through a "retention offer".

How to ask

One email, from the address you paid with, saying you want a refund. That is the whole process. Refunds are issued within 5 working days to the original payment method. Depending on your bank it can take another few days to appear.

What happens to your licence

Your key is revoked when the refund is issued, and the tools go back to the free tier. Anything you already generated is yours to keep and use.

The honest exceptions

  • After 14 days we are usually still happy to help — write to us and explain. It is a small business, not a machine.
  • Sharing or reselling a key voids the refund, and the licence.
  • Repeat buy-and-refund across multiple purchases will be declined.

Why the policy is this generous

Because a freelancer in Lagos or Cotonou taking a chance on a [PRICE] tool from someone they have never met is taking a real risk with real money. Making that risk reversible is the least the business can do. It is also, quietly, the reason people buy at all.

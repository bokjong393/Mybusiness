The short version

  • We do not ask you to create an account to use the free tier.
  • What you type is sent to our AI provider to produce your draft. It is not stored in a database by us and is not used to train models.
  • Your drafts are saved in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • We never sell your data. There is nobody to sell it to and it would be a terrible business.

What we process, and why

The text you type into a generator

Sent to Anthropic (our AI provider) over an encrypted connection so it can produce your draft, then streamed back to you. We do not write it to a database. Anthropic processes it as our sub-processor under their commercial terms; API inputs are not used to train their models. Their privacy policy: anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

Practical advice: paste what the generator needs and no more. It does not need your client's phone number, bank details or ID number to write you a proposal.

Your drafts and settings

Stored in your browser's local storage on your own device — your last 8 drafts, your licence key, your language, currency and theme. We cannot read them. Clearing your browser data deletes them permanently, and we cannot recover them.

Your IP address

Used in memory, for the length of a request, to count free generations so one person cannot use the whole day's capacity. It is not stored in a persistent log by us. Our hosting provider, [HOSTING PROVIDER], keeps standard server logs under its own policy.

Payment information

Handled entirely by [PAYMENT PROVIDER(S)]. We never see or store your card number. We receive your email address and the fact that you paid, so we can send your licence key.

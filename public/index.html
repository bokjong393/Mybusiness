Why it is different

It refuses to lie for you

Most AI writing tools will happily invent "10+ years of experience" and three fake case studies. The first time a client checks, your reputation is gone.

Ayoka will not fabricate a credential, a client name, a testimonial or a result. If you have no proof yet, it builds authority from your method and your point of view instead — and tells you plainly that one real number would convert better.

It also refuses the phrases that get freelancers deleted: just checking in, I hope this email finds you well, passionate about, circling back. Every single time.

Every response ends with

⚠️ Before you send this

  • What it had to guess because your brief was thin
  • The questions to ask the client first
  • What you must verify or replace
  • The one change that would improve it most

Questions

Do I need an OpenAI or Anthropic account?

No. You paste your brief, you get your draft. Nothing to install and no API keys to manage.

Is my client information stored?

What you type is sent to the AI model to generate your draft, and it is not written to a database or used to train anything. Your drafts live in your browser until you clear it. Full detail in the privacy policy.

Will a client be able to tell it was AI-written?

Not if you do the last step. Every response ends with the specific things you should change, verify or replace. The draft gets you 85% of the way; the last 15% is you, and it is the part that sounds like you.

Does the French actually read like French?

It is written as professional Francophone West African business French, not a word-for-word translation of English idiom. Headings, structure and all.

How do I pay from Nigeria or Benin?

Card, bank transfer and mobile money through the local checkout, or card and PayPal through the international one. Pick the currency above and the right checkout opens.

What happens after I pay?

You get a licence key by email — something like AYK-P0-9F3KQ2XM-7H2N4P8RQ1. Paste it into the app once and it is remembered on that device.

Is the work agreement a real contract?

It is a plain-language agreement that is far better than the nothing most freelancers use, and it says so at the top. For high-value work, have a lawyer in your country review it. Same for the tax lines on invoices — confirm rates with a local accountant.

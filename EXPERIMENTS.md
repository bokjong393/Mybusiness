# EXPERIMENTS.md — Validation Log

**Rule:** an experiment without a pre-registered threshold is not an experiment, it is a hope.
Write the threshold **before** running it. Record the result even when it is embarrassing.

**Rule 2:** compliments are not demand. "This is great, I'll keep you in mind" is a **failure**,
not a soft success. The only passing signals are (a) a specific named upcoming project,
(b) a question about price, (c) money.

---

## E0 — Payment rail verification `BLOCKER — RUN FIRST`

- **Hypothesis:** the founder can receive an international payment from a foreign client.
- **Method:** open accounts (Payoneer / Grey / Geegpay / Flutterwave — verify which accept Nigerian
  freelancers today), then **actually receive a real payment** — ask a friend or family member
  abroad to send $1–$5, or complete a $5 marketplace transaction.
- **Threshold:** ✅ money lands in a Nigerian bank account and is withdrawable.
- **Failure condition:** no rail completes a payout → **stop all outreach**, solve this first.
  Selling before you can collect wastes the only thing you cannot make more of: warm buyers.
- **Status:** NOT STARTED — **highest priority in the business**
- **Result:** —

## E1 — Sample asset creation `Week 1`

- **Hypothesis:** a matched EN/FR sample pair substitutes for a commercial track record **and**
  proves the bilingual claim, which the English-only sample does not.
- **Method:** (a) fix the existing mobile money report — remove the "Economic Research Analyst"
  byline (D-003), remove "Final Version (Verified)" from the cover, re-verify every headline figure
  against its primary source; (b) produce the **French version** of the same report — research is
  already done, so marginal cost is hours; (c) publish both, link in LinkedIn Featured.
- **Threshold:** ✅ both language versions published and linked within 7 days.
- **Failure condition:** the French version takes more than 3 days → the bilingual claim is weaker
  than assumed, which would invalidate the core positioning. **Treat that as a thesis-level signal.**
- **Status:** NOT STARTED — existing EN sample REVIEWED and judged strong enough to build on
- **Result:** —

## E2 — Cold outreach message test `Weeks 2–3`

- **Hypothesis:** ICP-A (boutique consultancies + companies operating across the EN/FR line)
  responds to a bilingual-research offer from an unknown sender.
- **Method:** 60 personalised LinkedIn connection requests + follow-up messages. One offer, one
  message template with genuine per-person personalisation. Log every send.
- **Thresholds:**
  - ✅ **PASS:** ≥8 replies (13%), ≥3 describing a real current or upcoming need, ≥1 asking price.
  - ⚠️ **ITERATE:** 4–7 replies → message is weak, ICP probably right. Rewrite and re-run.
  - ❌ **FAIL:** <4 replies from 60 → **the ICP is wrong**, not the message. Change segment.
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Result:** —

## E3 — Discovery conversations `Weeks 3–5`

- **Hypothesis:** the problem is urgent and expensive enough to buy a solution for.
- **Method:** 10 conversations (call or written) with qualified respondents.
- **Thresholds:**
  - ✅ **PASS:** ≥5 describe the pain unprompted, ≥3 name a specific upcoming document or decision,
    ≥2 ask "how much."
  - ❌ **FAIL:** <3 name anything specific → the problem is real but not urgent. Reconsider segment.
  - ❌ **HARD FAIL:** enthusiasm with zero named projects → **treat as failed validation.**
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Result:** —

## E4 — Price test `Weeks 4–6`

- **Hypothesis:** buyers will pay $300+ for a localization project from an unproven provider.
- **Method:** quote a fixed price to 5 qualified prospects. No discounting. No free trials.
- **Thresholds:**
  - ✅ **PASS:** ≥1 pays a deposit within 14 days.
  - ⚠️ **SIGNAL:** ≥3 counter-offer on price → demand exists, positioning is too weak; fix proof, hold price.
  - ❌ **FAIL:** 5/5 go quiet after the number → price, proof, or problem is wrong. Diagnose before re-running.
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Result:** —

## E5 — Marketplace parallel test `Weeks 2–5`

- **Hypothesis:** buyers already searching for bilingual research/localization will hire an
  unproven provider with a strong sample.
- **Why:** solves three problems at once — buyer discovery, payment rails, and first review.
- **Method:** 30 tailored proposals on Upwork/Contra for EN⇄FR business and research work.
- **Thresholds:**
  - ✅ **PASS:** ≥3 interviews and ≥1 paid job within 30 days.
  - ❌ **FAIL:** 30 proposals, 0 interviews → profile/sample is not credible; fix the sample first.
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Result:** —

## E6 — Retainer conversion test `Month 3+`

- **Hypothesis:** project clients with continuous need will convert to a monthly retainer.
- **Method:** offer a retainer to the first 3 satisfied project clients.
- **Threshold:** ✅ ≥1 of 3 accepts. ❌ 0 of 3 → the buyers are episodic, not continuous;
  the recurring-revenue thesis needs a different segment (test consultancies specifically).
- **Status:** NOT STARTED
- **Result:** —

## Global kill criteria

Re-open Phase 3 and select a different model if, after **90 days of genuine effort**:

- 200+ personalised outreach messages have produced **zero** paid customers, **and**
- 15+ discovery conversations have produced **zero** named, funded, upcoming projects.

Do **not** kill the thesis for: slow replies, one bad week, or a quiet LinkedIn feed.
Do kill it for: consistent interest that never converts to money.

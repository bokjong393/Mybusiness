# Weekly module drill

Run one week of one course. Roughly 90 minutes of her time.

Read `data/learning-log.md` first. If that course has open weak spots from an
earlier week, open with a two-minute check on those before touching new material.

## The order, and why it is this order

1. **Summary first — 4 min.** Before the videos, not instead of them. It gives
   her a skeleton to hang the week on, and it is usually a required "view" item
   anyway. Have her write down the terms it names; those become the targets.

2. **Videos at 1.5× — about 30 min.** No neat notes. For anything with curves,
   she draws while watching and pauses only to draw, never to transcribe.

3. **Blank page recall — 12 min.** The step that does the work. She closes
   everything and produces the week's core from memory: definitions, formulas,
   the diagram, the key relationships, and at least one *why* question rather
   than a *what*. Then she checks against the summary. **What she could not
   produce is the real study list** — not what felt hard while reading.

4. **Practice assessment, attempt one — 15 min. Cold.** No notes, no scrolling
   back. These are ungraded with two attempts and the higher score recorded, so
   the score is worthless and the diagnosis is everything. Do not answer these
   questions for her before her attempt; it destroys the only honest signal she
   has. Every wrong answer *and every lucky guess* gets written down.

5. **Repair — 15 min.** Only the items she missed. Re-watch those segments
   specifically rather than the whole video again.

6. **Attempt two — 10 min.** Aiming for full marks. This is spaced repetition
   aimed exactly at her gaps.

7. **Close out — 4 min.** Mark the LMS items done. Submit her worst question to
   the live-lesson request form, which almost nobody uses and which buys tutor
   attention on precisely her gap.

## Then

Write the week's entry to `data/learning-log.md`: what was covered, what she
could not produce, and a revisit date two or three days out. Tell her the
revisit is five minutes and nothing more — the forgetting curve is steepest in
the first 48 hours, and that short return is worth more than another half hour
on the day.

## Stakes worth naming

CA1 and CA2 are 40 marks between them, and each one banked is worth 1.5 marks of
relief in the final. Below 10 of 40 an A stops being reachable at all. Say so
when it is relevant; do not repeat it every week.

## Rules that make the difference

1. **Never send the same letter twice.** Paragraph 2 is the whole letter. If it could be addressed to
   any company, it will be read as if it was.
2. **Find the hiring manager's name.** Search the company on LinkedIn, filter to Finance. A named
   letter gets read; "To Whom It May Concern" gets skimmed.
3. **Send as PDF**, named `Peace-Sossa-Cover-Letter-[[Company]].pdf`.
4. **If the application is an email**, put a short version of this letter in the email *body* — not as an
   attachment nobody opens — and attach the CV. Subject line: `Finance Intern — Peace Sossa`.
5. **Do not apologise for what you lack.** Never write "although I have no professional experience".
   State what you have and stop. The employer knows you are an intern; that is the point of the role.
6. **The 200L question.** If the posting asks for a graduate or final-year student and you are 200L,
   apply anyway, but address it in one confident line rather than hoping it goes unnoticed: "I am in my
   second year, and I am available [[N]] hours a week around a fully online course schedule." Being
   upfront about availability is what they actually care about.

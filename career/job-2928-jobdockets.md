## Vet it before you apply — 10 minutes, every time

This is not about this listing specifically; I have not seen it. It is what you do with **any**
aggregator listing before spending effort on it, and especially before sending personal documents.
Aggregators republish from many sources and do not verify each one.

- [ ] **The company exists independently of the listing.** Website, LinkedIn page with real employees,
      a registered address. Check the CAC register for a Nigerian company.
- [ ] **The listing appears on the company's own careers page too.** If the role only exists on the
      aggregator, email the company directly to confirm before applying.
- [ ] **The contact email is on the company's domain** (`@company.com`), not Gmail or Yahoo.
- [ ] **"Full time" is compatible with your studies.** A full-time internship alongside 200L coursework
      needs an explicit conversation about hours — settle it before you accept, not after.
- [ ] **The stipend is stated, or you ask.** An unpaid full-time role is a significant thing to take on;
      decide deliberately rather than by default.

**Hard stops — walk away, no exceptions:**

- 🚩 You are asked to **pay** anything: training fee, application fee, ID processing, equipment deposit,
  "seminar" fee. Legitimate employers never charge to hire you. This is the single most common job scam
  in Nigeria.
- 🚩 **BVN, full bank account details, or a card number** requested during application or interview. Your
  BVN is never needed to apply for a job. Account details come *after* a signed offer, for salary only.
- 🚩 An offer with **no real interview**, or a hire decided entirely over WhatsApp.
- 🚩 You are asked to **receive money and forward it on**, or to buy gift cards or airtime. That is money
  laundering, and the liability lands on you.
- 🚩 Heavy pressure to **decide today**, or a request for your **NIN, passport or utility bill** before an
  offer exists.

None of this means the listing is bad — it very likely is not. Run the checklist, and if it clears,
apply properly with a tailored CV.

## What changed, and why

**1. Certifications got their own section — this was the biggest miss.**
Your World Bank CV lists only the CFA certificate. But you also hold certifications in
**Tableau and Power BI**, **MySQL**, and **two Excel certificates** — and those are, almost word
for word, the tools Rayda's posting asks for. They were sitting on your LinkedIn and nowhere on
your CV. Adding them costs five lines and closes three stated requirements with evidence.

**2. Tools moved up, above education.**
World Bank convention buries a skills list at the bottom. A startup screening 200 applicants for
a data role scans for tool names first. Yours are strong — don't make anyone hunt.

**3. The research bullets got re-pointed from economics to data operations.**
Same work, different vocabulary. "Extracted and processed financial data" became "pull and clean
datasets from six sources that don't agree with each other, reconciling inconsistent definitions
and formats" — because Rayda's first listed duty is *"collection, cleaning and organisation of
data from different business systems."* You are describing their job description back to them
using your own real experience. That is not spin; it is translation.

**4. The VA role now leads with the numbers.**
Your World Bank version had the sales/expense tracking third. For Rayda it goes first — it's
business-metrics tracking and recurring reporting, which is duty two on their list. And
**"16 consecutive cycles"** is the single strongest line you have for a remote hire. It says: she
delivers, unsupervised, every month, without being chased. Keep it bolded.

**5. "Available fully remote" in the header.**
You're in Okpoma, not Lagos. Say up front that this is a feature, not a logistics problem.

**6. Abomey-Calavi kept — and it matters more here than at the World Bank.**
That year is where your **SQL databases, programming and algorithmics** came from. For an
economics-only CV it's background; for a data analyst role it's a credential. I kept your honest
disclosure but stated it neutrally: *"Completed first-year coursework; no degree awarded"* rather
than leading with what didn't happen.

**7. Two fixes worth making everywhere.**
- **Email:** your CV and LinkedIn both use `peace.sossa@gmail.com`. Use that one consistently —
  it's the more professional of the two addresses you have.
- **LinkedIn URL:** your CV shows `linkedin.com/in/peace-sossa-94201b361`, the auto-generated
  one. You have a custom URL — `linkedin.com/in/peacesossa`. Use it. The long one looks like an
  unfinished profile.

## One line to consider adding

If your mobile money study is published somewhere with a URL — Substack, LinkedIn article,
Medium, a PDF in Drive — **put the link directly in the research bullet.** A hiring manager who
clicks it and finds a sourced, dated piece of analysis with a limitations section has already
decided about you. If it isn't linkable yet, making it linkable is worth an hour, today.

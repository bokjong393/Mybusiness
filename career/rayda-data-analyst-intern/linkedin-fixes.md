# LinkedIn — fixes before you apply

Your profile is doing a job. It just isn't the job you're applying for.

Right now it reads as a **freelance research service page**: a headline stacked with keywords,
a summary that runs through a service menu, turnaround times, white-label terms, a
money-back guarantee, and a free-sample call to action. For winning research clients, that's
reasonable. For a hiring manager at Rayda, it reads as *"she has her own thing going and this
role is a stopgap"* — and that's the read that quietly kills applications.

**The honest tension, stated once:** you want to own a business. That's real and I'm not
talking you out of it. But you can't ask one profile to close freelance clients and reassure an
employer at the same time — they want opposite things. Pick which one the profile serves for the
next few weeks, and know that you're picking.

Everything below assumes you want it working for the job hunt. If you'd rather keep it as your
client-facing page, say so and I'll rework this in the other direction.

---

## Fix 1 — The summary (biggest impact by far)

**Cut:** the service menu, the "5-day turnaround", "white-label", "if fewer than 40 rows are
usable, you don't pay", and the free-sample CTA. Also fix the run-together sentences — the
current text reads *"…they can actually verify.Economics training…"* with no space, in several
places.

**Keep:** the verification discipline. That paragraph is genuinely excellent and it's the most
distinctive thing on your profile.

**Replacement draft:**

> I research West African markets so decision-makers can act on information they can actually
> verify.
>
> Every piece of research I deliver carries its sources — dated, linked, checkable in ten
> seconds. That discipline is the product. Anyone can pull the data; most people don't verify
> it. My July 2026 study on Nigerian mobile money found that a third-place regional rank by
> account count hides the lowest per-capita penetration in the region — a gap that only appears
> once you normalise the series and read the definitions carefully.
>
> Economics training at Miva Open University (CGPA 4.53/5.00), a first year of mathematics,
> programming and SQL at Université d'Abomey-Calavi, and 17 months of fully remote work.
>
> Roots in Nigeria and the Republic of Benin, so I read the francophone sources most analysts
> skip and write for anglophone readers who need them. Native in both.
>
> I work in Excel, SQL, Power BI and Tableau. Open to data analyst roles, remote.

Shorter, evidence-led, and the last line tells a recruiter what to do with you.

## Fix 2 — The headline

Currently six keyword blocks separated by pipes. It's long enough that LinkedIn truncates it in
search results, so the end is invisible where it matters most.

> **Economics Undergraduate · Data & Market Research, EN/FR · Excel, SQL, Power BI, Tableau**

Tools in the headline is deliberate — that's what a recruiter's search matches on.

## Fix 4 — Rewrite the VA entry using your CV version

Your LinkedIn describes this role as documents, presentations and correspondence. Your CV
describes it as **tracking sales and expenses, reconciling cash, and 16 consecutive on-time
monthly reports.** The CV version is far stronger and it's the one that matters for a data role.
LinkedIn is currently underselling seventeen months of your own work.

**Add these two lines to the top of that entry's description:**

> • Track distribution sales and expenses, reconcile cash against records, and report monthly
> results to the Director.
> • Delivered Nigeria's monthly activity report on time for 16 consecutive reporting cycles.

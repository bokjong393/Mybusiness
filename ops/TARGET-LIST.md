# Building the Target List — 100 Agencies

The goal is 100 qualified agencies with a named human and a reachable channel. Budget ~4 hours. **This is the asset that makes week 1 possible** — outreach without it is staring at a blank sheet.

---

## Where they are

| Source | What to do | Yield |
|---|---|---|
| **Clutch.co** | Filter: service = *Lead Generation* or *Email Marketing*, size = 2–9 and 10–49, location = US/UK/CA/AU. Every listing has a website and often a named founder. | 30–40 |
| **Sortlist · DesignRush · GoodFirms · Agency Spotter** | Same filters. Overlaps with Clutch but surfaces different firms. | 15–25 |
| **LinkedIn company search** | Search the strings below, filter company size 2–10 and 11–50. Then find the founder from the company page's "People" tab. | 30+ |
| **LinkedIn people search** | Search job titles directly — gets you the decision-maker without a second lookup. | 20+ |
| **r/agency · r/leadgeneration · r/sales** | Sort by top posts this month. Active posters who run agencies are pre-warmed — they already talk publicly about this exact problem. | 5–10 |
| **Upwork** | Search jobs for "lead list", "prospect research", "list building". A client posting this job **has the problem and a budget right now.** Filter "No Hires" for the easiest entries. | 5–15 |
| **Cold-email tool communities** | Facebook/Slack groups for Instantly, Smartlead, Apollo. Membership is almost entirely agencies. | 10+ |
| **Sales podcast guest lists** | Agency founders go on podcasts. Guest pages are pre-qualified lists with names attached. | 5–10 |

## Search strings — copy these directly

**LinkedIn company search:**
```
"lead generation agency"
"outbound agency"
"demand generation agency"
"appointment setting"
"SDR agency"
"cold email agency"
"B2B lead gen"
```

**LinkedIn people search** (goes straight to the buyer):
```
("founder" OR "co-founder" OR "managing director") AND ("lead generation" OR "outbound")
```

**Google X-ray for LinkedIn profiles:**
```
site:linkedin.com/in/ ("founder" OR "co-founder") ("lead generation agency" OR "outbound agency")
```

**Google for agency sites:**
```
intitle:"lead generation agency" (US OR UK OR Canada OR Australia)
"we book meetings for" "B2B" -jobs -indeed
"appointment setting agency" "case study"
```

---

## Qualification — all four gates must pass

1. **Has paying clients.** Case studies, testimonials, or client logos on the site. No clients → no budget.
2. **Runs outbound.** Mentions cold email, SDR, appointment setting, or "we book meetings." Inbound/SEO-only agencies don't have this pain.
3. **One reachable decision-maker.** Founder-led, roughly under 15 people. Two names on the About page is ideal.
4. **Already spends on research.** They list list-building as a service, or have posted for a researcher/VA. **You are a substitution, not a new budget line.**

### Disqualify immediately
- 30+ employees — procurement and vendor onboarding will beat you on paperwork
- Solo freelancers — no budget, and they do this work themselves
- Visible in-house data team — pain already solved internally
- No website, or a website that hasn't been updated in two years

### One nuance worth knowing
An agency that **sells lead lists** is not a competitor — it is one of your best prospects. They need *supply*, and they resell at a markup. A white-label supplier relationship with a list-selling agency can be the largest single client on your books. Do not skip them.

---

## Method — 4 hours, in this order

1. **Hour 1 — Clutch.** Filters on, work down the listings. Agency name, website, country, size. Don't chase contacts yet; volume first.
2. **Hour 2 — LinkedIn.** Run the company searches, then the people searches. Founder name and profile URL.
3. **Hour 3 — fill the gaps.** Directories, Reddit, Upwork, communities. Get to 100 rows.
4. **Hour 4 — qualify and enrich.** Apply the four gates, cut failures, find emails for those that pass. Note **their niche** in its own column — that's what determines which pool rows they get.

**Their niche is the most important field in the sheet.** It is the difference between "here are 3 companies" and "here are 3 companies in *your* market," which is the difference between a reply and silence.

## Tracking

Use `templates/target-list.csv`. Log every contact the day you send it — the reply rate by channel is the single most valuable number the first month produces, and it is unrecoverable if you don't record it as you go.

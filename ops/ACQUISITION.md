# Customer Acquisition — Phase 12

**Principle: never depend on one platform.** A LinkedIn restriction, a domain blacklisting, or a subreddit ban must be able to cost us one channel without costing us the business. Three channels run in parallel from week one, even when one is clearly outperforming.

---

## Channel 1 — LinkedIn (primary at launch)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cash cost** | $0 |
| **Time cost** | ~3 min per contact |
| **Speed** | Fast — replies in 24–72 hrs |
| **Volume ceiling** | ~100–150 connection requests/week before throttling |
| **Expected reply** | 8–15% (higher than email: identity is visible, which builds trust) |
| **Scalability** | Low — hard platform limits |
| **Best for** | The first 10 customers |

**Why it leads for this founder:** it is the channel where being a real, visible person *helps*. A profile with a face, a clear specialism, and posted work partially neutralizes the location penalty that anonymous cold email amplifies. Trust is highest exactly where identity is most visible.

**Method:** search `"lead generation agency" OR "outbound agency" OR "demand generation"` filtered to 2–10 employees and target countries → connection request with the 300-character note → deliver 3 free signal rows on acceptance → soft offer.

**Risk:** account restriction from aggressive connecting. Mitigation: stay under 20/day, always personalize, never automate with a scraper tool.

---

## Channel 2 — Cold email (primary at scale)

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cash cost** | ~$8/month (domain + Workspace) |
| **Time cost** | ~3 min per contact after pool is built |
| **Speed** | Fast, but needs a 2-week domain warm-up first |
| **Volume ceiling** | 30–50/day per inbox once warm |
| **Expected reply** | 5–8% |
| **Scalability** | High — add inboxes to add volume |
| **Best for** | Getting from 2 clients to 6 |

**Warm-up schedule — do not skip this.** Days 1–3: 5/day. Days 4–7: 10/day. Week 2: 20/day. Week 3+: 30–50/day. A new domain sending 100 on day one lands in spam, and you will wrongly conclude the offer failed when only the deliverability did.

**Rules:** plain text only · no images or tracking pixels · one link maximum · 50–125 words · always a real, easy unsubscribe. Send from a domain that is *not* your future main brand domain, so a burn does not cost you the brand.

**Risk:** deliverability collapse. Mitigation: warm up properly, keep lists tight, stop immediately if bounce rate exceeds 3%.

## Channel 3 — Communities and marketplaces

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Cash cost** | $0 |
| **Time cost** | ~30 min/day |
| **Speed** | Slow — 4–8 weeks to first client |
| **Volume ceiling** | Low but compounding |
| **Expected conversion** | Low volume, high trust, best close rate of the three |
| **Scalability** | Medium — reputation compounds |
| **Best for** | Inbound that survives when outbound channels break |

**Where:** r/agency, r/leadgeneration, r/sales · agency-owner Facebook and Slack groups · Upwork, filtered to "No Hires" clients (new clients have no incumbent vendor and are the easiest first win).

**Method:** answer questions about prospecting and signals with genuinely useful, specific answers. Never pitch. Publish one free mini-list per week as a public post ("12 companies that posted SDR roles this week"). Let people come to you. This is slow and it is the only channel that produces inbound.

**Risk:** self-promotion bans. Mitigation: give away far more than you ask for, and follow each community's rules exactly.

---

## Channel 4 — Referrals (activates at client #2)

Zero cost, highest conversion, but nothing to activate until there is a happy client. Ask once, immediately after a delivery the client praised. Offer a free 3-row sample to whoever they name — same mechanic, warmer entry.

## Channel 5 — Content (month 4+, not before)

A weekly public "signals roundup" post or newsletter. Deliberately deferred: content before evidence is procrastination in a productive costume. It becomes worth building once we know from real clients which signals actually convert — at which point we are publishing insight, not opinion.

---

## Allocation and sequencing

| Phase | LinkedIn | Email | Communities |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Weeks 1–2 | 70% | 10% (warm-up only) | 20% |
| Weeks 3–6 | 45% | 40% | 15% |
| Month 3+ | 30% | 45% | 25% |

**Rebalance monthly on measured cost-per-conversation, not on preference.** Track reply rate per channel separately — they will diverge, and the divergence is one of the most valuable things this experiment produces.

## Kill criteria

Kill a channel after 150 contacts through it with under 2% reply. Do not nurse a dead channel out of sunk-cost attachment; move the hours to a live one.

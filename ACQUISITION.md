## PART 1 — Profile rewrite (do this before sending one message)

Every outbound message sends the recipient to the profile. A weak profile wastes every message
sent before it is fixed. This is a 90-minute job with the highest ROI of anything this week.

### Headline

> **West Africa market research, in English and French | Market-entry & fintech briefs for
> companies, investors and NGOs | Nigeria · Benin · WAEMU**

Why this: it states the service, the differentiator, the buyer, and the geography — and it carries
the keywords people actually search (*West Africa*, *market research*, *market entry*, *French*,
*fintech*, *WAEMU*). It claims no seniority, so nothing invites a background check (D-003).

**Delete** "Economic Research Analyst" as a self-assigned title. Describe the work, not the rank.

### About section — draft

> I research West African markets and write the briefs that companies, investors and development
> organisations use to make decisions — in English and in French.
>
> Most market intelligence on this region stops at the language border. Reports on Nigeria and
> Ghana are everywhere; credible, English-readable analysis of Benin, Togo, Côte d'Ivoire and
> Senegal is scarce, and the primary sources — central bank circulars, statistics offices,
> regulatory gazettes — are in French. I grew up across that border, between Nigeria and the
> Republic of Benin, and I work natively in both languages.
>
> **What I produce**
> · Market Snapshot — one specific question answered in 5–8 pages, 5 business days
> · Market Brief — full market structure, players, regulation, outlook, 15–25 pages, 10 days
> · Bilingual Brief — the same, written natively in English and French
> · Research retainers for consultancies needing continuous bilingual desk capacity
>
> **How I work:** every figure carries a named institutional source. Limitations are stated, not
> hidden. Projections are labelled as scenarios, never as forecasts. If a question can't be
> answered honestly with the data available, I'll tell you that instead of padding the page count.
>
> My published sample — a market analysis of Nigeria's mobile money sector, drawing on CBN, NIBSS,
> EFInA, IMF and GSMA data — is in the Featured section below. It is the standard I work to.
>
> Working on a West Africa decision? Message me with the question you're trying to answer.

### Featured section

1. Mobile money report — **English**
2. Mobile money report — **French** *(the pair is the proof; one alone is not)*

## PART 2 — Content strategy

### Content pillars

| # | Pillar | Purpose | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The francophone gap** — what's happening in Benin, Togo, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal that anglophone readers never see | **The differentiator.** Nobody else in the feed can do this. | 30% |
| **2** | **West African data, explained** — one figure, what it actually means, why the obvious reading is wrong | Authority | 25% |
| **3** | **How research is done** — sourcing, methodology, spotting bad data, why limitations sections matter | Credibility; converts sceptics | 20% |
| **4** | **Market-entry practicalities** — licensing, regulation, distribution realities across the border | Buyer-intent content | 15% |
| **5** | **Case notes & offer** — what a brief contains, what a client asked, what I found | Conversion | 10% |

**Deliberately excluded:** motivational content, career advice, hustle posts, engagement bait
("agree?"), and anything aimed at students. Those grow followers who will never buy (D-007).

### Post types and how to use them

| Type | Structure | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| **Contrarian reframe** | Common belief → the data → why the belief is wrong → what it means | Pillar 1, 2. Highest reach. |
| **Data insight** | One number → context → the non-obvious implication | Pillar 2 |
| **Research note** | "I went looking for X and found the data doesn't exist" | Pillar 3. Builds trust fast. |
| **Teardown** | A public report or claim, examined for method | Pillar 3 |
| **Practical explainer** | Steps, requirements, costs of doing a thing in a specific market | Pillar 4 |
| **Case note** | An anonymised client question and how it was answered | Pillar 5 |
| **Lead-gen post** | Publishes real value, ends with a low-friction ask | 1 per 8 posts, maximum |

## PART 4 — Outreach system

### Building the target list (free — no Sales Navigator)

LinkedIn search, People filter, then save names to a spreadsheet. Search strings to run:

- `"market entry" AND ("West Africa" OR Nigeria OR Senegal OR Benin OR "Côte d'Ivoire")`
- `"Country Manager" AND ("West Africa" OR Francophone)`
- `"Head of Expansion" OR "Expansion Lead" OR "Business Development" + Africa`
- `"Managing Partner" OR "Research Director" + consultancy + Africa`
- `"Programme Manager" OR "Chargé de programme" + (Benin OR Togo OR Sénégal)`
- French-language variants: `"étude de marché" Afrique de l'Ouest`, `"développement commercial" Afrique`

**Searching in French is an advantage almost nobody in your competitive set exercises.** Do it.

**Qualify before adding:** does this person plausibly own a West Africa decision, and could they
approve $500 without asking anyone? If not, don't add them.

**Target:** 100 named prospects in week 1, then 100/week thereafter.

### Volume and limits

- **15–20 personalised invitations per day**, five days a week.
- LinkedIn caps weekly invitations and periodically restricts personalised notes on free accounts.
  **Check what your account currently allows.** If notes are limited, send the plain invite and
  send the real message after acceptance.
- If your acceptance rate drops below ~20%, stop and fix the profile or the targeting. Do not push
  volume through a broken funnel — that's how accounts get restricted.

### Message sequence

**Message 1 — connection request (≤300 characters, must reference something specific)**

> Hi [Name] — saw you're leading [company]'s expansion into [market]. I research West African
> markets in English and French, mostly on the francophone side. Would be glad to connect.

**Message 2 — 1–2 days after acceptance. Give something. Ask for nothing.**

> Thanks for connecting, [Name].
>
> Since you're looking at [market] — I published a market analysis of Nigeria's mobile money
> sector recently (CBN, NIBSS, EFInA, IMF data, both EN and FR). The section on regional
> penetration might be relevant to what you're weighing. Happy to send it over, no strings.
>
> Out of curiosity — is the francophone side of the region on your roadmap, or are you focused on
> Nigeria and Ghana for now?

**Message 3 — 5–7 days later, only if they replied. A real question, not a pitch.**

> That's interesting — [specific reference to their reply].
>
> Most teams I speak to hit the same wall in WAEMU markets: the regulatory and market data exists,
> but it's in French and scattered across BCEAO circulars and national statistics offices. Is that
> what you're running into, or is it something else?

**Message 4 — soft offer, only after they have described a real problem.**

> If it's useful, that's the kind of question I answer for people — a short brief, 5–8 pages, one
> specific question, five working days, $200. Sourced properly, and in French too if you need the
> local version.
>
> Want me to put together what that would cover for [their question]? No obligation either way.

### Engagement-based outreach (highest conversion, lowest volume)

When someone comments on a post: reply publicly with something substantive first, wait a day, then
message referring to **their actual comment**. They have already signalled interest — this
converts far better than cold, and there are only ever a handful, so it costs almost nothing.

### Ethical rules — non-negotiable

- **No automation tools, no scrapers, no bulk senders.** They violate LinkedIn's terms and get
  accounts permanently restricted. The whole business dies with the account.
- **Never send the same message twice.** If it can't be personalised, don't send it.
- **Maximum two follow-ups on silence, then stop.** Silence is an answer.
- **"No" ends the sequence permanently.** No re-approaching in three months.
- No fake flattery, no invented common ground, no "I came across your profile and was impressed."
- No engagement pods, comment rings, or reciprocal-like schemes.

**The test:** would you be comfortable if this person screenshotted your message publicly?
If not, don't send it.

## PART 5 — Weekly operating cadence

| Day | Focus | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | 20 invitations · 1 post · reply to all comments | 4 |
| Tue | 20 invitations · 1 post · follow-up messages · 5 marketplace proposals | 5 |
| Wed | **Delivery day** — client work only, no selling | 8 |
| Thu | 20 invitations · 1 post · discovery calls | 5 |
| Fri | 20 invitations · 1 post · proposals out · **weekly review written down** | 5 |
| Sat | Sample/asset production (French version, checklist, next report) | 4 |

**~100 invitations, 4 posts, 5 proposals and one written review per week.** Log every message.
An unlogged funnel cannot be diagnosed — you'll never know whether a bad week was the message,
the targeting, or the offer.

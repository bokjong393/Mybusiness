# The First $100 — Phase 8

**Goal:** one stranger pays $147. Not a beautiful business. Not a brand. One payment.
**Deadline:** 21 days from start.
**Budget:** under $40.

Everything in this file is deliberately under-engineered. If you find yourself designing a logo, building a website, or registering a company, you have left the plan.

---

## The five answers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| **Who do I sell to?** | Founders of 2–15 person B2B lead-gen / outbound agencies in the US, UK, Canada, Australia |
| **What do I sell?** | A "Pilot Pack" — 50 companies matching one client ICP, each with a verified buying signal, the signal date, a source link, and a named contact |
| **What do I charge?** | **$147**, one-off, with a guarantee: fewer than 40 usable rows and they don't pay |
| **Where do I find them?** | Clutch.co and agency directories · LinkedIn search · r/agency and r/leadgeneration · agency Facebook and Slack groups · Upwork job posts |
| **How many do I need?** | **150–200 contacts** for 1–2 sales, at a conservative 6% reply rate |

## Week 0 — setup (one day, maximum)

**Do these five things and nothing else.**

1. **Payment rail.** Open Payoneer or Grey. This gives you USD account details a client recognizes. **Your domiciliary account is where money lands, not what you show a client.** Never write the words "wire transfer to my Nigerian bank" to a prospect — it converts a sale into a security conversation.
2. **Domain + email.** ~$12/year domain, Google Workspace ~$7/month. Start sending at 10–15/day and ramp; a new domain blasted at 100/day will land in spam and you will misread the failure as "the offer doesn't work."
3. **LinkedIn profile.** Headline: *"I find B2B companies with live buying signals — for outbound agencies."* Not "Economics student." Nobody buys a student; everybody buys a specialist. This is not deception — it is describing the service you actually sell.
4. **One Google Sheet template.** Columns: Company · Website · Signal · Signal Date · Source URL · Contact Name · Title · Email · Why Now. That is the entire product.
5. **A payment link.** Payoneer request-a-payment, or a plain invoice. Test it works before you need it.

**Do not build:** a website, a logo, a company registration, a pitch deck, a CRM, or software of any kind.

## Week 1 — build the pool, start touching

### Days 1–3: build the signal pool (~12 hours)

Research **120 companies** showing a live signal that they need outbound help. Sources, all free:

| Signal | Where to find it |
|---|---|
| Hiring an SDR / BDR / "growth" role | LinkedIn Jobs, Indeed, company careers pages |
| Recently funded (seed–Series B) | Crunchbase free tier, TechCrunch, funding newsletters |
| New VP Sales / CRO / Head of Growth | LinkedIn "changed jobs" filters, company announcements |
| Publicly complaining about pipeline | LinkedIn posts, r/sales, X |
| Expanding to a new market | Press releases, company blogs |

Record each in the template. **Every row must have a source URL and a date.** A row without a citation is worthless — the citation is the product.

This pool is reusable inventory. It is the reason the next step takes minutes instead of hours.

### Days 3–7: first 60 contacts (~4 hours)

- 30 LinkedIn connection requests with the note from `SALES.md`
- 30 emails, ramping from 10/day
- Each gets **3 rows from the pool**, matched to their niche, never repeated between agencies
- Per contact: 3–4 minutes

**Day 7 checkpoint:** 60 contacted, expect 3–5 replies. If zero replies from 60, stop and change the subject line and the first sentence before sending more. Do not simply send more of a message that isn't working.

---

## Week 2 — volume and conversations

- **90 more contacts** (~6 hours), same method
- Follow-up touch on day 3 and day 7 for everyone from week 1
- Reply to every response within a few hours — speed is a differentiator that costs nothing
- On a positive reply: send 3 more free rows, then quote $147

**Running total: ~150 contacts, expect ~9 replies, ~4 conversations.**

## Week 3 — close and deliver

- Convert conversations to pilots. Ask once, plainly: *"Want me to run the first 50?"*
- On a yes: confirm the ICP in writing, send the payment link, deliver in **5 business days or fewer**
- Deliver early if you can. Early delivery on a first order is worth more than a perfect one delivered on time.

---

## Delivery — how to actually produce 50 rows

1. Confirm the ICP precisely: industry, company size, geography, and **which signal types matter to them**.
2. Pull a candidate set from free tiers (Apollo free, LinkedIn search, Crunchbase free, job boards).
3. For each candidate, find a signal from the **last 30 days**. No signal, no row — drop it and move on.
4. Verify the signal is still true. **This step is the entire value.** AI will confidently report signals that expired months ago; checking is the human job you are being paid for.
5. Find the right contact and email. Verify format.
6. Write one "Why Now" line per row — the sentence the agency could paste into an email.
7. Read the sheet as if you were the buyer. Delete anything you would not personally send.

**Target: 6 hours for the first pack.** It will take eight. That is fine — the second takes five, the fourth takes three.

## Proof to collect (this is the real output of the first $100)

The money is almost incidental. What you are actually buying with these three weeks:

1. **A screenshot of the payment.** Every future prospect's real question is "has anyone else paid you?" This answers it permanently.
2. **A testimonial**, asked for within 24 hours of delivery while goodwill is at its peak. Ask for specifics: *"How many of the 50 were usable, and what would you have paid for that?"*
3. **A redacted sample file** — the single most powerful asset you will own. It replaces every credential you don't have.
4. **Reply-rate data by channel and message** — tells you where to spend month 2.
5. **Verbatim objections.** Every one goes into `SALES.md`. These are worth more than the $147.

## What "failure" looks like at day 21, and what it means

| Outcome | Diagnosis | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 150 sent, < 4 replies | Message or ICP is wrong | Rewrite the subject line and first sentence. Try recruiters (Plan C) as an alternate buyer. |
| Replies but no conversations | The free sample isn't impressive enough | Send 10 rows instead of 3 to the warmest 20 prospects |
| Conversations but no sales | Offer, price, or trust | Drop to $97, or offer the first 25 rows free against a $147 commitment for the next 50 |
| Sold but delivery took > 10 hrs | The unit economics don't work yet | Fix the process before selling a second one |
| **Someone paid** | **The hypothesis survived** | Go to `plans/VALIDATION.md` and prove it repeats |

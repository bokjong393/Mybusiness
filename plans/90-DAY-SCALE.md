# 90-Day Scale Plan — Phase 17

**Precondition: do not start this plan unless day 30 produced at least one paying customer.** Scaling an unvalidated offer multiplies a mistake.

---

## Days 31–45 — Convert proof into recurring revenue

**Revenue goal:** $500 MRR + $300 one-off · **Customer goal:** 2 retainers, 4 pilots sold

| Focus | Actions | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Convert pilots to retainers** | Offer the retainer within 48 hrs of every successful delivery, while satisfaction is highest | ≥ 40% pilot→retainer |
| **Lead with proof** | Every message now references the testimonial and offers the redacted sample | Reply rate up vs month 1 |
| **Raise volume** | 250 contacts across the 15 days | 250 sent |
| **Cut the weak channel** | Kill any channel below 2% reply after 150 contacts | Hours reallocated |
| **First price test** | Quote $197 to new prospects. Existing clients keep $147. | Does close rate hold? |
| **Product improvement** | Add "suggested opening line" per row — the most requested upgrade in early conversations | Client feedback |

**Automation this window:** sheet template + formatting macros; follow-up sequencing. Nothing else.
**Hiring:** none.
**Software:** none.

**Checkpoint day 45:** if MRR is $0 but pilots are selling, the product is a one-off, not a subscription. That is a real finding — record it and consider repositioning around volume of one-offs rather than forcing a retainer that buyers don't want.

---

## Days 46–60 — Reach the stated goal

**Revenue goal:** $1,000 MRR — **this is the six-month target, hit at day 60** · **Customer goal:** 3–4 retainers

| Focus | Actions | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Third and fourth client** | 250 more contacts, now with two testimonials | 2 new retainers |
| **Delivery speed** | Drive 50 rows from 6 hrs to 3 hrs using the SOP | ≤ 3 hrs/pack |
| **Retention** | Monthly one-page "what changed in your market" note to every client | Month-2 retention 100% |
| **Referral engine** | Ask every satisfied client once | ≥ 1 referred lead |
| **Distribution experiment** | Publish a weekly public signals list in one community | Any inbound at all |

**Automation:** email verification and enrichment pipeline. ICP intake form.
**Hiring:** none yet — but write the SOP as if you were handing it over tomorrow.
**Software:** still none.

**Checkpoint day 60:** with $1,000 MRR the original goal is met. **The correct move here is to stop and think, not to accelerate reflexively.** The real question is whether to keep adding clients (linear, capped around $3–4k solo) or start migrating toward Plan B's recurring structure. Log the decision in `DECISIONS.md`.

---

## Days 61–90 — Choose the second act

**Revenue goal:** $1,500–2,000 MRR · **Customer goal:** 4–5 retainers with month-3 retention above 50%

| Focus | Actions | Success measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Retention diagnosis** | Measure month-3 cohort retention honestly | ≥ 50% = the model holds |
| **Price migration** | New clients at $297 pilot / $650 retainer | Close rate holds above 25% |
| **Plan B pilot** | Offer bid/tender monitoring to 30 SMB contractors, using the same proof-first mechanic | ≥ 1 paying tender client |
| **Delegation test** | Train one assistant on the SOP for a single delivery step | Quality holds without founder |
| **Inbound asset** | Weekly public signals post becomes consistent | 1+ inbound lead/month |

**Automation:** full delivery checklist per signal type; invoicing.
**Hiring decision:** hire one part-time research assistant **only if** MRR ≥ $2,000 and delivery exceeds 60 hrs/month. A Nigeria-based assistant at $250–400/month is the second arbitrage in this business — but only against a written SOP.
**Software decision:** build only if all four Phase 11 gates are met (3+ retained clients, $2,000+ MRR, 20+ repetitions of an unchanged step, 10+ hrs/month saved). **At day 90 this will most likely still be a no.** That is the correct answer, not a failure.

---

## The day-90 strategic fork

By day 90 the data will point one of three ways. Decide deliberately; do not drift.

| If the data says | Then | Because |
|---|---|---|
| Retention ≥ 50%, clients expanding | **Scale the service.** Hire, raise prices, target $5k. | The model works; add capacity. |
| Retention < 50%, but pilots keep selling | **Reposition as a high-volume one-off product.** Higher price, no retainer pretence. | Forcing subscription on a transactional buyer burns goodwill. |
| Tender pilot outperforms prospect research | **Migrate to Plan B.** | Structural recurrence and a real accumulating moat beat a bigger commodity business. |

---

## What must be true at day 90 to continue

- [ ] ≥ $1,500 MRR
- [ ] ≥ 3 clients retained past month 2
- [ ] Delivery ≤ 3 hrs per 50 rows
- [ ] ≥ 1 acquisition channel with a repeatable, measured conversion rate
- [ ] A written SOP another person could execute
- [ ] Assumptions in METRICS.md replaced by measured numbers

**Fewer than four checked → stop scaling and fix the business.** More clients cannot repair a broken model; they only make it break louder.

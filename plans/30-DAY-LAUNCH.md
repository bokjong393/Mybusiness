# 30-Day Launch Plan — Phase 16

**One goal for 30 days: get a stranger to pay.** Every day below produces either evidence or revenue. If an activity produces neither, it is busywork and should be cut.

**Time budget:** 20–30 hrs/week. **Cash budget:** under $40 total.

**Standing daily rule:** reply to every prospect message within 4 hours. Response speed costs nothing and beats most competitors.

## Day 30 decision

| Result | Verdict | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 2+ paid, 1 retainer signed | **Strong pass** | Go to the 90-day plan. Raise outreach volume. |
| 1 paid, no retainer | **Weak pass** | Run one more 200-contact cycle. Change nothing else yet. |
| 0 paid, 3+ serious leads | **Near miss** | Problem is real, offer is wrong. Fix the offer only. |
| 0 paid, < 3 serious leads | **Fail** | Decision tree in VALIDATION.md. Likely change buyer, not skill. |

## Things explicitly forbidden in the first 30 days

Building a website · designing a logo · registering a company · writing software · buying courses · optimizing the LinkedIn profile more than once · reading about business instead of contacting agencies · redesigning the Sheet template · "researching competitors" for more than one hour total.

**Every one of these feels like work and produces no evidence.** The only two activities that count this month are *contacting strangers* and *delivering research*.

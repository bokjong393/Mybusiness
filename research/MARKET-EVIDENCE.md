## Payment rails (Nigeria → international clients)

The practical 2026 options are Payoneer, Grey, Geegpay (Raenest), Cleva, Wise, and Deel. Direct wires to a Nigerian domiciliary account work only "in limited cases." Payoneer provides virtual USD/EUR/GBP account details that appear to a client as a normal local bank account. Geegpay is recommended above $500/month for FX rates and same-day withdrawal. Standard practice is to run two rails, not one.

**Conclusion for us:** the domiciliary account is a settlement destination, not a client-facing payment method. A client must never be asked to "wire funds to a Nigerian bank" — that sentence loses deals. Set up Payoneer (or Grey/Geegpay) so the client sees ordinary account details or a payment link.

**Conclusion for us:** a $450–650/month retainer is far below the price of every alternative in this market. We are not competing on being cheap-and-worse; we are structurally cheaper because of where we live.

## AI commoditization of research services — the thesis-defining finding

"List-building as a standalone service has been commoditized by AI, but agencies are still paying humans — just for research insight, strategy, qualification, and validation rather than data compilation." AI handles volume: research, enrichment, personalization, follow-ups. Humans handle accounts where deal size justifies the time. 69% of B2B buyers still prefer to validate AI-generated information with a human.

**Conclusion for us:** never sell "a list." Sell a *verified, cited, dated buying signal*. The citation is the product. This single distinction is the difference between a $3/hour commodity and a $30/hour service.

## Cold email benchmarks — what sizes the whole plan

2026 B2B average: 27.7% open, 3.43% reply. 5–10% is good; above 10% is elite. Advanced personalization reaches 18% vs ~9% for generic templates. Lists under 50 recipients average 5.8% reply vs 2.1% for large blasts. Emails of 50–125 words perform best.

**Conclusion for us:** we plan on 5–8%, not 12%. Small, tight, deeply relevant batches beat volume. Every projection in METRICS.md uses the conservative end.

## Agencies as buyers of outsourced delivery

Agencies that outsource 40–60% of service delivery grow 2.3× faster than peers while holding 18–22% higher margins. Agencies outsource first where work "requires specialized expertise, creates bottlenecks, or fluctuates heavily with client demand."

**Conclusion for us:** prospect research is precisely a bottleneck that fluctuates with client demand. This is a pre-qualified buying motive, not one we have to create.

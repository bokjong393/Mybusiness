# Client Questions Log

**The single most valuable file in this repo.** Everything I sell later is written from it.

## The one rule

Record **their exact words**. Not my summary, not a cleaned-up version, not a translation.
If she said « les gens n'ont pas la patience d'attendre », write that — not "customers are
impatient." My paraphrase is worth nothing; their phrasing is the sales copy.

## Format

```
### [Date] — [Business], [City] — [FR/EN]
**Asked / said:** "..."
**Context:** what we were talking about
**Tally:** #N time I have heard this
```

## What to watch for

- **The same worry in both languages** — that is a real pattern, not a personality.
- **A question I could not answer** — that is a gap in my own offer. Write it down and fix it.
- **What they call things.** They do not say "AI assistant" or "chatbot". Whatever word
  they use is the word that goes in my next message.

# Week 1 — Working Materials

Everything needed to start Monday. No more analysis.

| File | Use |

## The week in four steps

1. **Build the list.** 40 businesses — 20 francophone (Cotonou, Abidjan, Dakar), 20
   anglophone (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt). Only ones showing a visible signal: unanswered
   comments, "DM for price", slow replies.
2. **Send 20 messages,** between 7pm and 9pm, one at a time, each with a real detail.
3. **Log every reply** in their exact words.
4. **Target: 5 real conversations. Zero sales.** Anyone who tries to buy, say yes — but
   chasing sales now wrecks the research.

## Kill criteria, decided in advance

| After 40 messages | Diagnosis | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 paid, <5 conversations | The **offer** is wrong | Change the vertical, not the price |
| 0 paid, 10+ interested | The **close** is wrong | Fix the demo and the price conversation |
| 2+ paid | It works | Move to the asset engine |
| Paid, but 3x the time estimated | The **scope** is wrong | Cut deliverables before cutting price |

## The thing to remember while doing it

None of this is unique, and it does not need to be. It works because you are reachable, you
work in both languages, and you will do a ₦40,000 job that nobody with an office can afford
to take.

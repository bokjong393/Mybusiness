Rates & method (you can disagree with my numbers)

There is no single objective price for unpaid work. PAY ME uses replacement cost: roughly what it would cost to buy comparable services on the market. Replacement cost is used instead of opportunity cost so that two people doing identical housework get identical valuations, regardless of what they earn elsewhere.

Use your own AI key (optional)

PAY ME works completely without AI — the tap-chips and the built-in classifier handle everything. Adding a key only improves the free-text reading and writes you a sharper job title.

Your key is stored only in this browser and sent only to the provider you pick. It never reaches this site’s servers, because this site has none. No key is committed to the repository. If you deploy the optional api/interpret function, visitors need no key at all.

# Semester syllabus map

Every week of every course, from the Course Overview table in each Study/Course
Guide. This is what the guides are *for*: they map the term, they do not teach it.

Each course runs twelve study sessions, one per teaching week, each carrying its
own assignment. Exams begin 28 November.

No guide is published for **SSC 202** or **GST 212**, so those are not listed.

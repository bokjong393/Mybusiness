# The First 30 Days

The goal of month one is **not** income. It is to find out whether anyone will pay me
before I spend three months building something.

Assumed budget: under ₦20,000. Assumed time: 10–15 hours a week around coursework.

---

## Week 1 — Talk to people, sell nothing

- [ ] Build a list of **40 small businesses**: 20 anglophone (Lagos, Abuja, Port
      Harcourt) and 20 francophone (Cotonou, Abidjan, Dakar). Instagram and WhatsApp
      Business catalogues are the easiest hunting grounds.
- [ ] Prioritise businesses that visibly reply slowly — the pain has to already exist.
- [ ] Send **20 messages**. Not a pitch. One question: *"How do you handle customer
      messages when you're asleep or in class?"*
- [ ] **Target: 5 real conversations. Zero sales expected.**
- [ ] Start `client-questions.md`. Write down their exact phrasing, not my summary of it.

## Week 2 — Build one thing, give it away twice

- [ ] Set up a WhatsApp AI assistant for an invented boutique. Make it good.
- [ ] Screen-record it answering a customer at 11pm. Under 40 seconds. This video is the
      product demo, in both languages.
- [ ] Offer **2 free setups** from the week-1 conversations, in exchange for a filmed
      testimonial — agreed in writing *before* starting.
- [ ] Deliver both. Time myself honestly. That number sets all future pricing.

## Week 3 — Charge money

- [ ] Go back to the remaining 20 businesses with the testimonials and the video.
- [ ] Price at **₦75,000**, stated explicitly as a starter rate for the first three
      clients. A stated reason for a low price protects the future price.
- [ ] **Target: 2 paid clients.**
- [ ] Test the payment rail properly: take a real payment and **withdraw it** before
      trusting it with anything larger.

## Week 4 — Turn the work into the asset

- [ ] Reread `client-questions.md`. The recurring questions are the course outline —
      written by the market instead of guessed at by me.
- [ ] Write the free French lead magnet: **"10 prompts ChatGPT pour vendre plus sur
      WhatsApp."** Two pages, real prompts, real examples. Not a teaser.
- [ ] Publish it. Collect WhatsApp numbers.
- [ ] **Target: 50 numbers.** This list is worth more than the month's revenue.

---

## Kill criteria — decide now, while I am unemotional

After 40 outreach messages:

| Result | Diagnosis | Action |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| 0 paid, fewer than 5 real conversations | The **offer** is wrong | Change the vertical, not the price |
| 0 paid, but 10+ interested conversations | The **close** is wrong | Fix the demo and the pricing conversation |
| 2+ paid | It works | Move to the asset engine |
| Paid, but each took 3x my estimate | The **scope** is wrong | Cut deliverables before cutting price |

The one outcome to refuse is drifting into month two with no data and a half-built
course. If week 3 produces nothing, that is information, and it cost four weeks instead
of four months.

## The only metrics that matter this month

1. Real conversations started
2. Testimonials filmed
3. Naira actually received **and withdrawn**
4. WhatsApp numbers collected

Followers, views, and likes are not on this list on purpose.
